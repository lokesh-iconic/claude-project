ACME CORPORATION — DATA SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE POLICY
Policy Number: SEC-2025-001 | Version 2.1 | Effective: February 2025

=== Section 1: Purpose and Scope ===

1.1 Purpose
This policy establishes the minimum security standards and compliance requirements for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who access Acme Corporation's information systems, data, or facilities.

1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all forms of information, whether digital or physical, including but not limited to: customer data, employee records, financial information, intellectual property, source code, and internal communications.

=== Section 2: Data Classification ===

2.1 Classification Levels
All data must be classified into one of four levels:

Level 1 - PUBLIC: Information intended for public consumption (marketing materials, press releases, public documentation).

Level 2 - INTERNAL: Information for internal use that would not cause harm if disclosed (internal newsletters, non-sensitive meeting notes, general operational procedures).

Level 3 - CONFIDENTIAL: Information that could cause competitive disadvantage or legal liability if disclosed (customer lists, financial projections, product roadmaps, employee compensation data).

Level 4 - RESTRICTED: Highly sensitive information requiring maximum protection (PII/PHI subject to regulatory requirements, encryption keys, security credentials, M&A documents, trade secrets).

2.2 Handling Requirements
- PUBLIC: No special handling required
- INTERNAL: Must not be shared outside the organization
- CONFIDENTIAL: Encrypted in transit and at rest; access logged; shared only on need-to-know basis
- RESTRICTED: End-to-end encryption; multi-factor authentication required; access requires manager and Security team approval; all access logged and audited quarterly

=== Section 3: Access Control ===

3.1 Principle of Least Privilege
Access to systems and data shall be granted on a least-privilege basis. Employees shall receive only the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.

3.2 Authentication Requirements
- All systems must enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters with complexity requirements
- Password rotation: every 90 days for standard accounts, every 30 days for privileged accounts
- Biometric authentication is required for physical access to data centers

3.3 Access Reviews
- Quarterly access reviews for all systems containing CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data
- Access must be revoked within 4 hours of employment termination
- Privileged access (admin, root) requires annual recertification by the CISO

=== Section 4: Incident Response ===

4.1 Incident Classification
- P1 (Critical): Active data breach, ransomware, system-wide outage
- P2 (High): Suspected unauthorized access, failed security controls
- P3 (Medium): Policy violation, phishing attempt (contained)
- P4 (Low): Security advisory, minor configuration issue

4.2 Response Timeline
- P1: Immediate response, CISO notified within 15 minutes, incident commander assigned within 30 minutes
- P2: Response within 1 hour, security team investigation within 4 hours
- P3: Response within 4 hours, investigation within 24 hours
- P4: Response within 24 hours, resolution within 5 business days

4.3 Reporting Obligations
- GDPR: Data protection authority notification within 72 hours of confirmed breach
- CCPA: Affected California residents notified "in the most expedient time possible"
- SEC: Material cybersecurity incidents reported within 4 business days (Form 8-K)
- Internal: All security incidents logged in the Incident Management System (ServiceNow)

=== Section 5: Compliance Requirements ===

5.1 Regulatory Frameworks
Acme Corporation maintains compliance with:
- SOC 2 Type II (annual audit)
- ISO 27001 (triennial certification with annual surveillance audits)
- GDPR (for EU customer data)
- CCPA (for California resident data)
- HIPAA (where applicable to healthcare clients)

5.2 Training Requirements
- All employees: Annual security awareness training (mandatory, tracked in LMS)
- Engineering: Secure coding practices training (annually)
- Management: Data privacy and incident response training (annually)
- New hires: Security onboarding within first 5 business days

5.3 Audit and Monitoring
- Continuous monitoring of all production systems via SIEM (Splunk)
- Annual penetration testing by an independent third party
- Quarterly vulnerability scans of all externally facing systems
- Monthly review of access logs for RESTRICTED data systems

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and GlobalTech Industries ("Client")
Effective Date: March 1, 2025
Agreement Number: MSA-2025-0142

=== Article 1: Scope of Services ===

1.1 The Provider agrees to deliver the software platform known as "Acme Analytics Suite" (the "Platform") to the Client, including installation, configuration, training, and ongoing support as described in Exhibit A.

1.2 The scope of services includes:
(a) Deployment of the Platform on Client's cloud infrastructure (AWS or Azure)
(b) Data migration from Client's existing analytics tool (up to 500GB)
(c) Configuration of up to 25 custom dashboards
(d) Training for up to 50 end users (remote sessions, recorded)
(e) 12 months of technical support (Tier 1 and Tier 2)

1.3 Any services not explicitly listed in this Agreement or Exhibit A shall be considered out of scope and subject to a separate Statement of Work (SOW) and additional fees.

=== Article 2: Term and Termination ===

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of 36 months ("Initial Term").

2.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within 30 days of receiving written notice.

2.4 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days' written notice, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to 25% of the remaining contract value.

=== Article 3: Fees and Payment ===

3.1 The Client shall pay the Provider the following fees:
(a) Platform License Fee: $180,000 per year, billed annually in advance
(b) Implementation Fee: $45,000, billed upon project kickoff (non-refundable)
(c) Training Fee: $15,000, billed upon completion of training sessions
(d) Support Fee: Included in the Platform License Fee for Tier 1 and Tier 2. Tier 3 (on-site) support is billed at $250/hour.

3.2 All invoices are due within 30 days of receipt (Net 30). Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.3 Fees shall increase by no more than 5% annually upon each Renewal Term, with 60 days' advance written notice.

=== Article 4: Intellectual Property ===

4.1 All intellectual property rights in the Platform, including source code, documentation, and derivative works, shall remain the exclusive property of the Provider.

4.2 The Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Platform during the term of this Agreement, solely for Client's internal business purposes.

4.3 Any custom configurations, dashboards, or integrations developed specifically for the Client ("Custom Work") shall be owned by the Client, provided all fees have been paid in full.

=== Article 5: Data Protection and Security ===

5.1 The Provider shall implement and maintain industry-standard security measures, including encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), to protect Client data.

5.2 The Provider shall comply with all applicable data protection laws, including GDPR, CCPA, and any regulations applicable to Client's industry.

5.3 In the event of a data breach affecting Client data, the Provider shall notify the Client within 48 hours of discovery and cooperate fully in investigation and remediation.

5.4 Upon termination, the Provider shall return or securely destroy all Client data within 30 days, at Client's election, and provide written certification of destruction.

=== Article 6: Service Level Agreement (SLA) ===

6.1 The Provider guarantees 99.9% uptime for the Platform, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

6.2 Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed 4 hours per month and shall be performed between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM ET on Sundays, with 48 hours' advance notice.

6.3 SLA Credits:
- 99.5% - 99.9% uptime: 5% credit on monthly license fee
- 99.0% - 99.5% uptime: 15% credit on monthly license fee
- Below 99.0% uptime: 30% credit on monthly license fee

6.4 SLA credits shall not exceed 30% of the monthly license fee and are the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for downtime.

=== Article 7: Limitation of Liability ===

7.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, REGARDLESS OF THE CAUSE OF ACTION OR THE THEORY OF LIABILITY.

7.2 The Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client during the 12-month period immediately preceding the claim.

7.3 The limitations in this Article shall not apply to breaches of confidentiality obligations, data protection obligations, or intellectual property infringement.

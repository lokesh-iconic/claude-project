ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective January 2025

=== Section 1: Employment Policies ===

1.1 At-Will Employment
All employment at Acme Corporation is "at-will," meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice. No manager or representative of Acme Corporation has the authority to enter into any agreement for employment for any specified period of time.

1.2 Equal Opportunity
Acme Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other legally protected characteristic.

1.3 Probationary Period
New employees are subject to a 90-day probationary period during which performance, attendance, and cultural fit are evaluated. Benefits eligibility begins on the first day of the month following completion of the probationary period.

=== Section 2: Compensation and Benefits ===

2.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. If a payday falls on a company holiday, payment will be issued on the preceding business day. Direct deposit is required for all employees.

2.2 Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours worked in excess of 40 hours per workweek. Overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager. Unauthorized overtime may result in disciplinary action.

2.3 Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance coverage including medical, dental, and vision plans. The company covers 80% of the premium for individual coverage and 60% for dependent coverage. Open enrollment occurs annually in November.

2.4 401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees are eligible to participate in the company's 401(k) plan after completing 6 months of employment. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary, and 50% of contributions between 4% and 6% of salary.

=== Section 3: Time Off Policies ===

3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO accrues based on length of service:
- Years 0-2: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- Years 3-5: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- Years 6+: 25 days per year (2.08 days/month)
PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year. Unused PTO beyond the carryover limit is forfeited.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3.3 Company Holidays
Acme Corporation observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and the day after
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day

3.4 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must commence within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

=== Section 4: Workplace Conduct ===

4.1 Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

4.2 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Employees must maintain a dedicated workspace and reliable internet connection. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval.

4.3 Dress Code
Business casual attire is expected in the office. Client-facing meetings require business professional attire. Casual Fridays permit jeans and company-branded clothing.

4.4 Confidentiality
All employees must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) upon hire. Company proprietary information, client data, trade secrets, and internal communications must not be shared outside the organization without explicit written authorization.

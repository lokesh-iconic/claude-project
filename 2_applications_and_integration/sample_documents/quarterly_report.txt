ACME CORPORATION — QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT
Q2 2025 (April - June)

=== Executive Summary ===

Acme Corporation delivered strong results in Q2 2025, exceeding revenue targets by 12% and achieving a net profit margin of 18.3%. Total revenue reached $47.2 million, up from $41.8 million in Q1 2025, representing a 12.9% quarter-over-quarter growth. Operating expenses were held at $32.1 million, demonstrating continued cost discipline despite expansion into two new markets.

=== Section 1: Revenue Breakdown ===

1.1 Revenue by Segment
- Enterprise Solutions: $28.3M (60% of total revenue, up 15% QoQ)
- Small Business: $12.1M (26% of total revenue, up 8% QoQ)
- Consumer Products: $6.8M (14% of total revenue, up 11% QoQ)

1.2 Revenue by Geography
- North America: $31.2M (66%)
- Europe: $9.4M (20%)
- Asia-Pacific: $4.7M (10%)
- Rest of World: $1.9M (4%)

1.3 Recurring Revenue
Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) grew to $156M, up from $142M at end of Q1. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) was 118%, indicating strong expansion within existing accounts. Customer churn rate remained low at 2.1% quarterly.

=== Section 2: Operating Metrics ===

2.1 Customer Metrics
- Total customers: 4,230 (up from 3,890 in Q1)
- Enterprise customers (>$100K ARR): 187 (up from 172)
- Average deal size: $67,400 (up 9% from $61,800)
- Sales pipeline: $89M (up 22% from $73M)

2.2 Operational Efficiency
- Gross margin: 72.4% (up from 71.1% in Q1)
- Operating margin: 32.0% (up from 29.5% in Q1)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): $12,400 (down 8% from $13,500)
- CAC Payback Period: 11 months (improved from 13 months)

2.3 Headcount
Total employees: 612 (up from 580)
- Engineering: 245 (40%)
- Sales & Marketing: 156 (25%)
- Customer Success: 98 (16%)
- G&A: 113 (19%)

=== Section 3: Balance Sheet Highlights ===

3.1 Cash Position
Cash and equivalents: $82.3M
Short-term investments: $15.0M
Total liquidity: $97.3M (runway > 24 months at current burn rate)

3.2 Key Ratios
- Current ratio: 3.2
- Debt-to-equity: 0.15
- Free cash flow: $8.7M (Q2), $14.2M (H1 2025)

=== Section 4: Outlook ===

4.1 Q3 2025 Guidance
- Revenue: $50M - $52M (6-10% QoQ growth)
- Operating margin: 30-33%
- Headcount additions: 30-40 new hires (primarily engineering and sales)

4.2 Strategic Priorities
1. Launch self-service analytics platform (target: August 2025)
2. Expand APAC sales team with Tokyo office opening
3. Achieve SOC 2 Type II certification by end of Q3
4. Release API v3.0 with enhanced integration capabilities

4.3 Risk Factors
- Foreign exchange headwinds in EUR/USD may impact European revenue
- Increased competition in the SMB segment from two new entrants
- Potential regulatory changes in data privacy (EU AI Act compliance)
